# Demo image credits

All images come from [Unsplash](https://unsplash.com) and are used under the [Unsplash License](https://unsplash.com/license) (free to use, no permission needed, attribution appreciated). They were resized to at most 1600 px on the long side and recompressed as JPEG (sips). Nothing else was changed.

Photographer names were taken from the Unsplash listing pages when the images were picked; the photo pages themselves were not reachable by script (bot check), so check the source link if a name matters.

Rules used to pick them (DECISIONS #112): no religious imagery, no readable brand logos or club crests, no jerseys with names, no identifiable close-up faces, no watermarks, no heavy text. Football photos are used for football events only, concert photos for concerts only, and so on.
